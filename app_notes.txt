php class demonstration
following Dani Krossing at https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yrFr5PMdk2A
made this auth_system with Gemini 3.6 flash extended + Chatgpt 5.6 luna,
saves raw username and password (insecure) in users.json file.

run:
php -S localhost:8000

http://localhost:8000/   --(index.php shows signup form)

http://localhost:8000/signup-page.php   --(signup page)

http://localhost:8000/login-page.php  --(shows signin page)

http://localhost:8000/dashboard-page.php  --(shows after login)
